LELAMP SIMULATION: ACTIONS AND STATES
===================================

Run from LeLamp/:
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe vision_behavior.py
Quit: press q in the camera window, or close the camera/viewer window.
Directions refer to the unmirrored camera preview. Motion values are radians.

VISUAL ACTION PRIORITY (highest first)
PHONE > PEACE > POINT_LEFT/RIGHT/UP/DOWN > HAND > NEW FACE > HAPPY/ANGRY > IDLE
Higher-priority reactions can interrupt lower-priority movement smoothly.
A held phone or peace sign suppresses lower-priority events even after its
own animation finishes. Voice OFF overrides every visual lighting effect.

PHONE
  Detect a cell phone with YOLO confidence >= 0.50 on two fresh inferences.
  Wake if dimmed, say "Stop scrolling, please lock in twin."
  Allow a 0.35-second speech lead-in, shake yaw +/-0.18 for four cycles with
  0.25-second flashing segments, then return toward neutral over 0.5 seconds.
  Interrupts current/queued lower-priority speech, including jokes.
  Five-second cooldown plus disappearance latch; holding it does not repeat.

PEACE
  Index and middle fingers extended/separated, ring/little fingers curled.
  Three positive frames confirm. Dance: left, right, up, down, head twist
  both ways, neutral. Yaw/head +/-0.12; pitch +/-0.10; beats 0.40 seconds.
  Light stays on unless manually off. No disco music exists in this version.
  Four-second cooldown plus disappearance latch.

POINT_LEFT / POINT_RIGHT / POINT_UP / POINT_DOWN
  Index finger extended; middle/ring/little fingers curled. Three positive
  frames confirm. Diagonals and severely foreshortened fingers are rejected.
  LEFT: yaw +0.15. RIGHT: yaw -0.15. UP: pitch -0.10. DOWN: pitch +0.10.
  Smooth move for 0.55 seconds, hold one second, return over 0.60 seconds.
  One-second per-direction cooldown plus disappearance latch. A new direction
  waits for the current pointing animation to finish. Conflicting directions
  from two hands do not select a pointing action.

HAND
  A visible hand without a recognized peace/pointing gesture.
  Three frames confirm. Small yaw wiggle +/-0.08, then return to neutral.
  Four-second cooldown plus disappearance latch.

FACE
  Newly visible face; three positive frames confirm.
  Wake if needed, say "Hello there", nod and flash, return to neutral.
  Pitch sequence +0.12, -0.12, +0.06, neutral; nod segments 0.55 seconds.
  No repeated greeting while continuously visible. Leave and re-enter to rearm,
  subject to the four-second cooldown. There is no face identity tracking.

HAPPY
  FER+ happiness class: probability >= 0.65 and lead over runner-up >= 0.15.
  Four fresh positive expression inferences confirm; runs after face greeting.
  Friendly yaw +/-0.06 and upward pitch -0.06, then neutral. No added speech.
  Five-second cooldown plus expression disappearance latch.

ANGRY
  FER+ anger probability >= 0.25. Must rank first, or second behind neutral
  with a gap <= 0.15. Three fresh positives confirm; runs after face greeting.
  Gentle head tilt +0.08, downward pitch +0.06, light softened to 65%, neutral.
  Says "You seem angry. Let me lighten up your mood with a joke."
  Then a randomly selected online joke, ending with "Hahaha".
  Jokes are prefetched in the background; local backups cover loading/failure.
  Five-second cooldown plus expression disappearance latch.
  This is a visible-expression classification, not knowledge of inner emotions.
  The sensitive threshold can classify some neutral faces as ANGRY.

EXPRESSION UNKNOWN
  No usable face, an unsupported expression, or insufficient confidence.
  No expression action. SAD is no longer an action.

VOICE COMMANDS
  ON: "on", "turn on", "lamp on".
  OFF: "off", "turn off", "lamp off".
  Finalized Vosk recognition only; each word confidence >= 0.80.
  Input is normalized; unrelated/partial speech is ignored.
  OFF stays off until ON. Visual motion can still happen while manually off.
  Microphone pauses during lamp speech and briefly afterward to reduce feedback.

LAMP AND BEHAVIOR STATES
  IDLE: no active motion sequence; neutral control targets.
  ON: starts enabled at full brightness.
  DIMMING: after ten seconds without a newly accepted interaction, fades to
    20% brightness over two seconds. Continuous face presence does not reset it.
  DIMMED: idle brightness 20%.
  WAKING: new event restores brightness over 0.6 seconds before visual action.
  MANUAL OFF: user voice OFF; flashes, idle, and wake cannot enable the light.
  FLASH OFF: temporary off segment within face/phone reaction, not manual OFF.
  REACTION SOFTENING: ANGRY light multiplier 65%, restored at completion.
  PENDING ACTION: waiting for wake or a higher-priority visual condition to clear.
  COOLDOWN / WAIT FOR LEAVE: visible item already reacted to or cannot rearm yet.
  ACTIVE: PHONE, PEACE, HAND, FACE, HAPPY, ANGRY, or one of four POINT actions.
  Light, activity, voice, and motion states are separate and can coexist.

REARMING AND ACTIVITY
  Five fresh negative detector results clear presence/latch. Skipped inferences
  do not count. Expressions run every third frame; phone inference every second.
  New accepted visual actions and accepted voice ON/OFF reset the idle timer.

SYSTEM STATUS / EXIT
  Camera opening -> loading models -> waiting for frame -> Camera LIVE.
  Stale camera data (>1 second) cannot trigger new visual events.
  Voice: loading, listening, paused for speech, disabled, or unavailable.
  Camera/physics errors stop the run; normal exit returns toward neutral and
  turns off illumination. A physics error stops stepping rather than animating.

MANUAL TESTS (no camera required)
  python vision_behavior.py --manual phone
  Replace phone with peace, hand, face, happy, angry, point_left, point_right,
  point_up, or point_down. Phone and angry manual tests include speech.
  Manual tests exit after completion; other manual reactions do not play speech.

VALIDATION
  Automated geometry, priorities/latches, lighting, speech dispatch, joke fallback,
  and model control/physics checks are available in verify_*.py.
  Passing automated tests does not guarantee live gesture/expression accuracy.
